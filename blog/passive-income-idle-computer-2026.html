How to Earn Passive Income From Your Idle Computer in 2026

Turn your idle computer into a 24/7 income stream by renting your browser to AI agents.

Published July 17, 2026 · 6 min read

In 2026, your computer's most valuable asset isn't its GPU or CPU — it's its browser. AI agents need real browsers to interact with websites, and they're willing to pay for access to yours. Here's exactly how to set it up.

What Is Browser Rental?

Ceki Browser is a P2P marketplace where AI agents rent browser sessions from real people. You install an extension, set your availability, and get paid per minute while AI agents use your browser. You keep working normally — the rental runs in a sandboxed browser profile.

Step-by-Step Setup

1. Install Ceki Browser

Download Ceki Browser from browser.ceki.me and install it alongside your regular browser. It's a full Chromium-based browser with a built-in provider extension.

2. Configure Your Provider Profile

Set your preferences in the provider dashboard:

  • Availability hours: when your computer is idle (e.g., overnight, work hours)
  • Max concurrent sessions: how many AI agents can use your browser simultaneously
  • Geographic region: select which regions can rent your browser (some users prefer local renters)

3. Start Earning

Once configured, the browser handles everything automatically. AI agents rent your session, use it for their tasks, and you earn per-minute fees deposited to your account.

What AI Agents Do With Your Browser

Understanding how your browser is used helps you appreciate the value you're providing:

  • Research automation — AI agents collecting data from multiple websites
  • Form filling — automated form submissions across various platforms
  • Content monitoring — tracking changes on competitor websites
  • E-commerce operations — price comparison, inventory checking, order placement

Privacy and Security

You might worry about privacy — you're right to. Ceki Browser addresses this with:

  • Sandboxed sessions: renters cannot access your cookies, passwords, or personal data
  • Encrypted traffic: all data transmitted between renters and your browser is encrypted
  • No keylogging: providers cannot see what renters are doing in the browser
  • Instant kill switch: stop any session immediately from your provider dashboard

Is It Worth It?

For most people, browser rental won't replace a full-time income. But as passive income goes, it's uniquely attractive:

  • Zero effort: set it up once and forget about it
  • Zero risk: your personal data is never exposed
  • Zero marginal cost: your computer is already running
  • Scalable: add more browsers on more machines for more income
