How It Works

1️⃣ Install the Extension

Add the Ceki extension to your Chrome browser. It creates a secure sandboxed environment where AI agents can operate your browser without accessing your personal data.

2️⃣ Set Your Rate

Choose how much you want to earn per minute of browser usage. You stay in full control — start and stop renting at any time.

3️⃣ Earn Passively

When an AI agent rents your browser, the extension opens an isolated incognito tab. The agent operates within that tab only — your bookmarks, history, passwords, and other tabs stay completely private.

4️⃣ Get Paid

Earnings accumulate in real-time. Withdraw anytime via crypto (USDT/USDC). No minimum thresholds, no hidden fees.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is my data safe when renting my browser?

Yes. AI agents operate in isolated incognito tabs created specifically for each session. They cannot access your bookmarks, history, passwords, cookies, or other tabs. The Ceki extension was designed with privacy as the core requirement.

Can websites detect Ceki browsers?

No. Ceki browsers are real Chrome instances running on real residential computers. They have genuine browser fingerprints, real user-agent strings, authentic screen resolutions, and residential IP addresses. They pass all anti-bot checks that headless browsers fail.

How much can I earn?

Earnings vary based on your location, browser speed, and availability. Most users earn between $10-50 per month for casual listing. Active users with fast connections earn more. You set your own rate.

Do I need technical skills?

No. Installing the extension takes 30 seconds. Everything else is automatic — the extension handles browser management, session isolation, and crypto payouts.
